Before you book

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in after 2:00 pm. Check-out before 12:00 pm. Call if you need a late arrival.

Where do I book?

Online at Campspot, or call the office at (512) 793-3922.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are generally allowed — confirm the fee and cabin rules when you book. Do not leave pets unattended outside.

Can I bring a tent or pop-up?

No tents or pop-ups. RV sites are for self-contained rigs.

What hookups do RV sites have?

30- and 50-amp electric, water, and sewer. Waterfront sites sit about 15 feet from the water’s edge when the lake is up.

What if the lake is low?

Lake Buchanan rises and falls. Waterfront still means closest to the shore, but the water can recede. Ask us for a current photo before you travel if that matters for launching or swimming.

How is the Wi-Fi?

The property is serviced by Starlink. It is a lakeside park, not a downtown hotel. Do not plan a 4K film edit from a waterfront pad. Cell service varies by carrier.

Is this a quiet park?

Yes. Some sites are used by longer-term guests. Quiet hours are expected. It is a family resort, not a party marina.

How far from Austin?

About 90 minutes, depending on traffic and whether you come up 1431 or through Burnet.

Do cabins have full kitchens?

Honeymoon and lodges have kitchen setups (fridge, microwave, coffee; lodges add stove/oven). Bungalows have kitchen basics. Bring groceries from Burnet or Marble Falls.
