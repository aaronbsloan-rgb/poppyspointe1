Lake Buchanan & Hill Country

The lake is the main event. The rest of the weekend is close.

Sunrise on this page is from the property. Inks Lake / Marble Falls shots can still be added later.
Lake BuchananBoating, swimming, and some of the best striped bass water in Central Texas. Ramp on site.
Inks Lake State ParkA short drive. Pink granite, hiking, and another swimming hole when you want a change of shoreline.
Longhorn CavernUnderground tour south of Burnet. Good midday plan when it is 100° outside.
Spider MountainBike park and lift near the lake. Useful if teenagers are in the van.
Marble Falls & BurnetGroceries, tacos, antiques, and a real coffee stop before you come back to the cabin.
Wineries & backroadsHill Country tasting rooms and wildflower drives in spring. Llano and Kingsland are easy day loops.

Make the lake your home base.
